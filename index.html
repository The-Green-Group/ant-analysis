Ant Analysis

1. Competitor Quality Efficiency

Market leader Aberdeen Laundry Services published 133 posts to gain 246 followers (1.8 followers per post), whereas The Green Group gained 173 followers from just 15 posts (11.5 followers per post).

This demonstrates that our strategy achieved 6.24 times greater follower capture efficiency per post than our highest-volume competitor without spamming the market.

2. Local Market Penetration

Over 72% of our followers hold key management or executive titles, with over 34% located directly in Glasgow and Edinburgh across hospitality and laundry sectors.

In a Scottish market constrained to 150–200 key decision-makers, high local capture means growth naturally flattens, making targeted account nurture far more critical than broad follower acquisition.

3. Operational Content vs. Broad Reach

Data-heavy operational content delivered our highest engagement, with system breakdowns generating 156 clicks at a 33.4% engagement rate.

Conversely, broad video posts racked up 12,652 impressions but converted at under 1.9%, proving that senior B2B buyers engage with operational substance over consumer-style reach.

4. High Commercial Intent

Across the last three months, 141 unique page visitors generated a total of 487 content clicks on our profile.

This high ratio of 3.45 interactions per unique visitor proves that key decision-makers are repeatedly returning to inspect our services, establishing a prime audience for direct Account-Based Marketing.

Performance Summary (Predecessor vs. July Onward)

Since taking over in July, content output scaled 4x from 3 posts to 12, driving total impressions up by over 125% from 4,936 under predecessor to 11,143. This increased publishing frequency expanded overall brand visibility across Scotland while successfully maintaining high engagement, securing 540 total content clicks from key decision-makers.
